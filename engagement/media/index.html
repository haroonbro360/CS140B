What are the positives and negatives of hosting your videos on YouTube?

Positives: YouTube is free, easy to use, and it can handle large video files. It also makes it easy to share videos with other people. Negatives: Ads may appear, you have less control over the video player, and your videos depend on YouTube's platform

What is the most useful thing you can do with a transform?

The most useful thing is to move, rotate, scale, or change the appearance of an element on a webpage to make it more interesting.

What is the difference between a transform and a transition?

A transform changes an element's position, size, rotation, or shape. A transition makes that change happen smoothly over time instead of instantly.
