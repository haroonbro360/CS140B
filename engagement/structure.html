What goes in the header?

The section contains information about an HTML webpage, such as its title, metadata, and links to CSS or JavaScript.

What goes in the Nav? Can the Nav go in the header? Does it have to go in the header? Does the Nav contain links to another website?

The nav contains navigation links, can be placed inside or outside the header, and can contain links to other websites.

What goes in Main?

The main contains the main content of the webpage, such as text, images, articles, or other important information

What goes in the Footer?

The Footer contains information at the bottom of a webpage, such as copyright, contact information, and links.

HTML Validation
