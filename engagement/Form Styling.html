Answers and Questions

1. What are different ways to style your form?

We can change colors, borders, background, padding, margins, font size, and spacing. We can also use Flexbox and media queries.

2. Why does the professor like to use flex?

Flexbox helps organize labels and input fields. It makes it easier to align items and control spacing.

3. What makes for a good mobile form?

A good mobile form is easy to read and use on a small screen. It should have clear labels, enough space, and large buttons.
