Welcome to Laie

Laie is a beautiful town on the north shore of Oahu, Hawaii. It is known for its beaches, green scenery, and peaceful environment. Many people visit Laie to enjoy its natural beauty.

The Beauty of Laie

Laie has beautiful places to visit with family and friends. Visitors can enjoy the ocean, take pictures, and learn about Hawaiian culture. The town has a friendly and welcoming feeling.

The Laie Hawaii Temple is an important landmark in the town. It belongs to The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Its gardens and architecture make the area beautiful.

Relative Positioning

CSS positioning helps web designers move boxes around a page. Relative positioning moves an element from its normal position, but its original space remains reserved.

Absolute Positioning

Absolute positioning moves an element out of normal flow. It can be placed using top, bottom, left, and right values. It is positioned relative to its nearest positioned ancestor.

Fixed Positioning

Fixed positioning keeps an element in the same place on the screen while the user scrolls. It can be useful for small buttons or other elements that should remain visible.

Questions and Answers

1. What is normal flow in HTML?

Normal flow is the default way elements are arranged on a webpage. Block elements appear from top to bottom, and inline elements appear next to each other.

2. What does float do? Why do you need to clear the float?

Float moves an element to the left or right and allows text to wrap around it. Clear makes later content start below the float when needed.

3. What do absolute, fixed, and relative do?

Relative moves an element from its normal position. Absolute positions an element relative to its nearest positioned ancestor. Fixed keeps an element in the same place on the screen while scrolling.

4. How can you make nice navigation buttons for mobile devices?

Use CSS to make navigation links block elements, add padding, give them a background color, and make them wide enough to tap easily on a phone.
