Explore Beautiful Hawaii

Final Project Proposal

1. Title and Topic

My project title is Explore Beautiful Hawaii. My website will introduce people to beautiful places, beaches, food, and activities in Hawaii.

I chose this topic because Hawaii has many interesting places and a beautiful environment. I want visitors to learn more about Hawaii and discover places they can visit.

2. Five Content Pages

  1. Home: An introduction to Hawaii and the website.
  2. Beaches: Information about Waikiki Beach, Lanikai Beach, and Sunset Beach.
  3. Attractions: Information about Pearl Harbor, Diamond Head, and Polynesian Cultural Center.
  4. Food: Information about poke, loco moco, malasadas, and kalua pig.
  5. Activities and Attractions Table: Information about surfing, hiking, snorkeling, and swimming, along with a table comparing popular Hawaii attractions.

3. Table and Form Content

Table Information

My table will compare popular attractions in Hawaii. It will include:

  • Place name
  • Location
  • Activity
  • Estimated cost

Form Information

My form will collect information from visitors. It will include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Favorite Hawaiian island
  • Comments and suggestions

4. Content Collection and References

I will collect information by researching reliable websites about Hawaii. I will use these sources to learn about different beaches, attractions, food, and activities. I will write the descriptions in my own words. I will also find appropriate pictures from sources that allow people to use their images and give credit when required.

My references include:

  1. Go Hawaii - Official Hawaii Tourism Website
  2. Hawaii State Government - Official Government Website
